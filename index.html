About

I am a computational biologist specializing in plant genomics and multi-omics integration. Currently, I serve as a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Technical University of Munich (TUM), where I focus on advancing our understanding of cereal genomics through innovative computational approaches.

As CTO and Co-founder of Sentry Bio, I'm applying my expertise in computational biology to address critical challenges in biosecurity and biotechnology. Our mission is to develop cutting-edge solutions that bridge the gap between fundamental research and practical applications.

My work combines rigorous computational methods with biological insights to tackle complex problems in plant biology, genomics, and beyond. I'm passionate about leveraging data science, machine learning, and bioinformatics to drive scientific discovery and innovation.
